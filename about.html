About DoBi

An offline survival AI for Mac and Windows.

DoBi is an offline AI survival assistant for Mac and Windows. It was built because a family’s safety was dependent on a Wi-Fi signal that could vanish in a second.

It ships with over 20,000 pages of expert survival knowledge. You can add your own PDFs. It is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

DoBi OS is a downloadable ZIP (about 10.3 GB for Mac and about 11.7 GB for Windows). DoBi is a 1 TB portable SSD, hand-prepared for Mac or Windows, and it ships within 5 to 7 business days from Michigan, USA. DoBi Nitro is a custom-built mini PC. All three editions include the same DoBi software, AI models, and survival knowledge base.

Mac editions are built for Apple Silicon (M1 or newer). Windows editions need a 64-bit Intel or AMD PC.

DoBi is made by DoBi.

Questions: support@dobi-survival.com, or the contact page.
