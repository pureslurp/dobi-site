Be ready for anything

F-01
100% offline

No internet, no cloud, no phone-home. Everything runs locally on your machine. Your data stays yours.

F-02
Survival library

A powerful pre-trained AI model enhanced by over 20,000 pages of expert survival material. Upload your own PDFs to expand its knowledge with the references that matter most to you.

Download the ZIP or plug in the SSD. Either way, DoBi launches with a single click. Ongoing updates are included and delivered as simple file updates with step-by-step instructions.
