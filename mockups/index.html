DoBi Homepage Visual Directions

Four distinct visual approaches for the DoBi homepage. Each direction explores a different brand personality while maintaining trustworthiness and premium quality for a survival/preparedness product.

A. Field

Rugged, military-inspired aesthetic. Colors: olive drab (#4a5240), sand (#d4c5a0), off-white (#f5f3ed), burnt-orange accent (#c15a2e). Typography: Merriweather (serif headings) + IBM Plex Mono. Hard-edged, field-ready, with geometric shapes and strong borders.

View Full Mockup

B. Blaze

High-contrast, industrial aesthetic. Colors: charcoal (#2b2d2f), safety-orange (#ff6b00), warm white (#faf9f6). Typography: Inter (all weights). Bold, technical, with strong gradients and floating shadows. Emergency visibility with premium execution.

View Full Mockup

C. Topo / Outdoor

Topographic map-inspired with outdoor texture. Colors: cream (#f7f4ed), forest green (#2d5016), rust (#b54d28), sage (#8a9a7a). Typography: Lora (serif headings) + Source Sans 3. Subtle radial contour patterns, refined outdoor aesthetic.

View Full Mockup

D. Rescue / Clean

Clean, clinical emergency aesthetic. Colors: white (#ffffff), deep slate (#2c3e50), signal red (#e63946), signal yellow (#ffc300). Typography: Space Grotesk (headings) + DM Sans. Maximum breathing room, medical precision, high-visibility accents.
