FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about DoBi before you buy.

So DoBi (doh-bee) is ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT needs powerful cloud servers in massive data centers, huge compute resources, and constant internet. After setup, DoBi runs entirely on your own computer with much simpler hardware. Since DoBi doesn’t have access to kilowatts of energy and giant high-compute models running in those data centers, its responses are more focused and direct (specialized in survival knowledge) rather than as fast, long, or broadly creative as cloud AIs. Once you’re past initial setup, answers are private and don’t depend on the cloud or the grid. Perfect when it actually counts.

What's the difference between DoBi OS, DoBi, and DoBi Nitro?

DoBi OS is our most popular option: a downloadable ZIP file (about 10.3 GB for Mac, about 13 GB unzipped; about 11.7 GB for Windows, about 14.5 GB unzipped) containing the full DoBi software. Just download, extract, and click to run. DoBi is a 1 TB portable SSD with everything pre-loaded, shipped straight to your door. Plug it in and go. DoBi Nitro is a custom-built mini computer with DoBi pre-installed, built to order for users who want a dedicated, always-ready DoBi machine. All three editions include the same DoBi software, AI models, and survival knowledge base.

Does DoBi only know about survival?

No, DoBi knows about a wide range of topics. It's built on a powerful pre-trained AI model that already understands a wide range of topics. Although DoBi will generally try to help you with survival topics, you can ask it about anything you want.

What hardware do I need?

Mac: DoBi is built and tested for Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 chip or newer). It may run on older Intel-based Macs, but we haven’t tested it there, so we only recommend buying if you have an Apple Silicon Mac. You need a current macOS, at least 4 GB of RAM, and 8 GB or more is recommended for the best experience. DoBi auto-detects your hardware and picks the best AI model tier and uses the Mac's graphics hardware when available for faster responses.

Windows: DoBi requires a 64-bit Intel or AMD PC. We recommend 16 GB of RAM for the best experience, though DoBi will run on systems with less memory by automatically selecting an appropriate model tier.

If you are unsure about your system, contact us and we can help.

Does DoBi need the internet?

DoBi OS (the download) needs an internet connection the first time you launch it to confirm the license. After that, you can use it fully offline for normal use.

DoBi (SSD) and DoBi Nitro are built to run fully offline, even the first time you launch it. There’s no account or sign-in for day-to-day work.

What topics does DoBi cover?

DoBi is built on a powerful pre-trained AI model that already understands a wide range of topics. On top of that, it ships with over 20,000 pages of expert survival material spanning water purification, emergency medicine, first aid, foraging and wild plants, food preservation and canning, gardening, hunting and trapping, shelter construction, wilderness survival, navigation, military field operations, knots and ropework, nuclear preparedness, and much more. You can also upload your own PDFs to expand DoBi's knowledge with any reference material you choose.

What AI models are included?

Three tiers are included with every edition of DoBi: Full, Mid, and Lite. DoBi automatically selects the best one for your computer, but you can switch between them at any time. The Full model gives the most detailed answers and works best on newer or more powerful systems (Apple Silicon Macs or PCs with 16+ GB RAM). The Lite model is lighter and runs well on entry-level machines or when you have less memory.

What do I receive when I order?

It depends on which edition you choose. DoBi OS: you'll receive an instant download link for a ZIP file after purchase. Extract it and click to launch. The first time you open the app, you’ll need an internet connection to complete setup; after that you can use it offline. DoBi (SSD): you'll receive a high-quality 1 TB portable USB-C SSD with everything pre-loaded for your selected platform (Mac or Windows). We source from trusted brands like Crucial, Samsung, Kingston, Lexar, ADATA, or SanDisk, always USB 3.2 Gen 2 or better. Each unit is hand-prepared and ships within 5–7 business days from Michigan, USA. DoBi Nitro: you'll receive a fully configured mini PC, custom-built to your specifications. Contact us for details.

Can I use the SSD on more than one machine?

Yes, as long as all machines match your SSD's platform. Mac SSDs work on Macs with an M1 chip or newer. Windows SSDs work on 64-bit Intel/AMD PCs. Each SSD is prepared for one platform. Unplug and move the drive between compatible machines. DoBi is portable by design.

Can I add my own PDFs to the knowledge base?

Yes. DoBi has a built-in Knowledge Base section in the sidebar where you can drag-and-drop or click to upload any text-based PDF. Your uploaded files are automatically indexed and become part of DoBi's knowledge base immediately. No restart or rebuild needed. Add purchased survival manuals, medical references, field guides, homesteading books, or any other PDF reference material you want DoBi to know about. You can view and remove uploaded files at any time. All processing happens locally. Your files never leave your machine. Note that DoBi needs text-based PDFs; scanned image-only PDFs without OCR text won't be usable.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your questions, conversations, and uploaded files stay on your machine. There’s no account, no sign-up, and no tracking. DoBi OS only needs the internet for that first-launch setup; after that, everyday use doesn’t require a connection, and your chats and knowledge base aren’t sent to us or to a cloud service.

What is the return/refund policy?

If DoBi isn't working out for you, contact us within 30 days for a full refund. For DoBi (SSD) orders, we’ll provide a return shipping label at no cost to you; standard manufacturer warranties apply to the SSD hardware. For DoBi OS (digital download), we will refund you and revoke your license, so reach out and we'll make it right. DoBi Nitro refund terms are discussed during the inquiry process.

How do I get updates?

When an update is available for your version of DoBi, you'll be notified directly. You'll receive updated files along with step-by-step instructions — the process is straightforward and doesn't require internet on the target machine. Ongoing updates and improvements are included with your purchase at no extra cost. Major new versions of DoBi may be released separately in the future.

What if I have technical issues?

DoBi ships with a README file that covers common setup steps and typical problems. Start there if something isn’t working. If you’re still stuck, email us any time at support@dobi-survival.com. We’ll help you work through it.

Can I choose which SSD brand I get? (DoBi SSD)

We select the best SSD available based on current stock, quality, and value, always from trusted brands like Crucial, Samsung, Kingston, Lexar, ADATA, or SanDisk. We can’t guarantee a specific brand or model, but every SSD we ship is USB 3.2 Gen 2 or better, fast, reliable, and ready for your selected platform. Quality and performance are guaranteed.

How does DoBi Nitro work?

DoBi Nitro is a pre-built mini PC with DoBi fully installed and optimized. Each Nitro is custom-built to order. Contact us to discuss your needs, and we'll put together a build with pricing and lead times. Nitro units ship fully configured. Just plug in power and a monitor and you're ready to go.
